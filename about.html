Built for shoppers who want it done right, the first time.

Six Star Suppliers connects everyday buyers with vetted retailers and wholesalers across Kenya — with every product checked before it ever reaches the storefront.

Our story

Six Star Suppliers started as a small supply desk moving household goods and electronics to shops and individual buyers around Nairobi. Today, every product on the site passes through an approval step before it's listed — so what you see is what's actually in stock, at a price our team has checked.

How ordering works

Browse the catalog, add what you need to your cart, and check out with your delivery details and an M-Pesa payment. Our team verifies each payment against your order before it's confirmed and dispatched — you can follow every step from the Track My Order page.

Shipping & returns

We deliver countrywide. Delivery timing depends on your location and is confirmed once your order is verified. Every order carries a 1-year warranty; if something arrives faulty or wrong, reach out within 7 days and we'll sort it out.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay?

Pay via our Paybill number and paste the M-Pesa confirmation message at checkout, or arrange payment on delivery where available.

How long does verification take?

Most M-Pesa payments are verified within a few hours. You'll see the update on the Track My Order page.

Can I sell on Six Star Suppliers?

Yes — register as a retailer or wholesaler. Seller tools for listing products are being added to this site soon; in the meantime, reach out to us on WhatsApp to get set up.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the item, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund under the 1-year warranty.
