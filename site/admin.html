Categories

Parent Category → Category → Sub Category. Click a row's arrow to expand its subcategories. Products attach to whichever level has no further subcategories under it — use "Attributes" on those rows to define what sellers fill in. Set a marketplace commission % on any level; categories below it inherit that rate unless you give them their own.

Attribute Definitions

Reusable characteristics like Brand, Size, or Color. Mark an attribute as "Creates variants" if each value needs its own stock count (e.g. Size, Color) — otherwise it's just a single descriptive value on the product (e.g. Brand, Material).

Pending Review

Flash Sale runs daily 2:00 PM – midnight. Sellers submit at least 24 hours before the next 2:00 PM slot with their price, discount, stock allocation, and product details. Approving here schedules the product to go live automatically at 2:00 PM; it comes down at midnight or the moment its allocated stock sells out — whichever comes first — with no further admin action needed after approval.

STK Push Issues

Orders paid via M-Pesa STK Push that failed (wrong PIN, insufficient balance, cancelled, timeout) or are still waiting on M-Pesa's response. These can never be manually "confirmed" — only M-Pesa itself (via webhook, or the recheck below) can say a payment succeeded. Cancelling here releases reserved stock immediately instead of waiting for the automatic cleanup.

Seller Transaction Fee Tiers

Flat KSh fee charged to a seller, based on that seller's own subtotal within a single order. Ranges shouldn't overlap between active tiers — leave "Up to" blank on your top tier for an open-ended range (e.g. "2500 and above"). Editing this ladder only affects future orders; past orders keep the fee that was snapshotted at the time.

Marketplace Earnings

Marketplace commission is what Six Star keeps from confirmed sales; agent commission is what gets paid out to sales agents. Net earnings = commission − agent payouts. Defaults to confirmed payments only, since that's real money.
