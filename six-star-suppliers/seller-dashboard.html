Flash Sale

Daily 2:00 PM – midnight deals

How the Flash Sale works

Runs daily 2:00 PM–midnight. Submit at least 24 hours ahead with your Flash Sale price, stock allocation, and product details — the platform reviews and approves it, then it goes live automatically at 2:00 PM and ends the moment its stock sells out or midnight hits, whichever comes first.
